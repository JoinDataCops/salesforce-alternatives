## Why data quality matters more than platform choice

Salesforce implementations fail at a high rate, and CRM migrations fail even more often. The cause is rarely features. It's data.

Salesforce's Agentforce AI platform has a 3% to 27% hallucination rate in 2026. Adoption sits at 5.3% of Salesforce customers. The core issue: AI agents running on siloed, duplicate-ridden, inconsistently mapped enterprise data produce unreliable outputs. This isn't a model problem. It's a data problem.

The same issue affects every CRM migration. Moving from Salesforce to HubSpot or Zoho transfers your data to a new system. It doesn't clean your data.

## The data quality prerequisite

Before any CRM migration, teams should:

1. Export and audit all contact records for duplicates
2. Validate email addresses and phone numbers
3. Flag records with missing or non-consented opt-in data
4. Filter bot-generated and VPN-sourced leads
5. Reconcile account/contact relationships across systems
